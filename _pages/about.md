I am a [PhD Student](https://www.rit.edu/) at the [Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT)](https://www.rit.edu/), New York, where I started my PhD in Fall 2026 under the supervision of [Dr. Matthew Wright](https://sites.google.com/site/matthewkwright/), Professor and Chair of [Cybersecurity](https://www.rit.edu/computing/department-cybersecurity) at RIT. My research interests include Trustworthy Agentic AI, Deepfake Detection, Digital Media Forensics, Machine Learning Security, Multi-modal Learning, Large Language Models and Generative AI.

Previously, I was a [Junior Research Scientist](https://iub.ac.bd/directory/ccds?type=centers-and-institutes) at the [Center for Computational & Data Sciences (CCDS)](https://ccds.ai), Independent University, Bangladesh. I received my Bachelor's degree in CSE from [KUET](https://www.kuet.ac.bd/cse) in 2024. I have hands-on experience in academic research and teaching through roles as a Research Assistant and Lecturer/Teaching Assistant.

I am fortunate to have collaborated with and received mentorship from [Md Mofijul Islam](https://mmiakashs.github.io/) (Applied Scientist, Amazon GenAI), [Aman Chadha](https://www.aman.info/) (Google Deepmind), and [Sajib Mistry](https://staffportal.curtin.edu.au/staff/profile/view/sajib-mistry-d41c64f3/) (Associate Professor, Curtin University, Australia).

I am open to research collaboration with research groups, labs, and industry on topics related to Trustworthy Agentic AI, and Machine Learning Security.
